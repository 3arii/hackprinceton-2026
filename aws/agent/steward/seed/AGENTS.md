# Lake Carnegie: what the steward knows

## The lake
- 5 km reservoir in Princeton NJ, about 100-300 m wide, fed by the Millstone River and Stony Brook (enters at the west end near buoy 1).
- Four buoys cluster mid-lake by Lake Drive: buoy 1 west (nearest the stream mouth, usually murkier), buoy 3 easternmost.
- Baseline: USGS 01402000 Millstone River at Blackwells Mills, 15 km downstream. It lags the lake by roughly a day and reads 1-2 °C warmer in autumn.
- Typical November values: water 9-12 °C, turbidity 5-15 NTU (buoy 1 higher), salinity 0.12-0.18 PSU, microplastics 3-7 particles/L.

## Manager preferences
- Alert only when something needs action today; one clear alert beats several partial ones.
- Always say whether a sensor fault is plausible.
