# Microplastics spike

There is no public microplastics baseline, so compare buoys with each other and with their own history.

## Hypotheses to test
1. **Stormwater flush**: rain in the last 1–2 days washes tire and road particles in; turbidity rises at the same time; highest near inflows.
2. **Wastewater effluent**: treatment plants release fibres steadily; look for the buoy nearest their outfalls and their compliance history.
3. **Local source**: one buoy only, dry weather, nothing else moving.

## Thresholds
- Above 12 particles/L is high for this lake; a doubling from a buoy's own baseline is worth noting.

## Community signals
Community (Bluesky) can surface litter, dumping or sewage-overflow reports near the inflows.
